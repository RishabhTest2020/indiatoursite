Colva Beach

Relax on powder-white sands, enjoy thrilling water sports, and savor fresh Goan seafood along South Goa's coastline.

WhatsApp Us
Duration 3 Days / 2 Nights
Best Time November – March
Budget ₹8,000 – ₹20,000
Ideal For Beach Lovers, Water Sports & Families

Overview

Colva Beach is one of South Goa's oldest and most scenic coastal stretches. Spanning 2.4 kilometers of powdery white sand lined with swaying coconut palms, Colva offers beachside shacks, thrilling parasailing, jet skiing, and Portuguese heritage churches just a short stroll inland.

Typical Itinerary

Day 1

White Sands & Watersports

Spend your morning soaking in the sun and enjoying parasailing or banana boat rides. In the evening, dine on fresh tiger prawns at beachside shacks.

Day 2

Portuguese Churches & Sunset Walk

Visit the 17th-century Church of Our Lady of Mercy. Take a relaxing late-afternoon stroll down to Benaulim beach for a peaceful sunset.

Must-Try Experiences

Powder-White Sandy Coastline
Parasailing, Jet Skiing & Speedboat Rides
Historic Church of Our Lady of Mercy
Authentic Goan Fish Curry & Bebinca
Lively Evening Beach Shacks & Live Music
