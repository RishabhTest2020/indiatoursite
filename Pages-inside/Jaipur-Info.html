Overview

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is world-famous as the Pink City. Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, it is a magnificent blend of Rajput tradition and urban planning. The city features hilltop fortifications like Amer Fort, the iconic 953-casement Hawa Mahal, and bustling bazaars overflowing with jewels, block-printed textiles, and pottery.

Typical Itinerary

Day 1

Amer Fort & City Palace

Explore the majestic Amer Fort on a hilltop overlooking Maota Lake. In the afternoon, explore the sprawling City Palace complex and Jantar Mantar astronomical observatory.

Day 2

Hawa Mahal & Local Bazaars

Photograph the stunning honeycomb facade of Hawa Mahal. Shop for handcrafted souvenirs, lac bangles, and textiles in Johari and Bapu Bazaars.
